Full-Service HVAC Care for Olympia Homes and Businesses

Heating and cooling around here covers a lot of ground: older gas furnaces in established neighborhoods, high-efficiency heat pumps in recent construction, ductless mini-splits in additions and converted garages, and packaged units on commercial buildings. Our team works on every one of those systems. We have years of field experience, and we've learned how the local climate, the housing stock, and local code affect the way a system runs and fails.

A normal week for our residential team includes capacitor and contactor replacements, flame sensor cleanings, refrigerant leak repairs, blower motor swaps, and at least one full system replacement or ductless addition. Getting More Years From Your HVAC System in Olympia

A little routine care makes all the difference between a system that lasts and one that breaks down in the middle of a heat wave. Our advice for homeowners is a short list: a new filter every one to three months, two feet of clearance around the outdoor condenser, a spring rinse of the condenser coil, and a professional tune-up every spring and fall, so small issues get caught early.

Age matters too. Most central air conditioners run 12 to 15 years, furnaces 15 to 20, and heat pumps 10 to 15 with regular service. If your system is past that window and showing symptoms, such as rising energy bills, rooms that never quite reach temperature, or a service call every season, a planned replacement is almost always cheaper and far less stressful than an emergency one in the middle of a cold snap.

The earlier you report a comfort problem, the cheaper it usually is. A weak capacitor is an easy, low-cost part to replace, yet that same capacitor, neglected through a full season, can ruin the compressor and turn a simple fix into a full system replacement. Save our number in your contacts today so you can reach us fast the moment something seems off in your home. A quick call saves time, money, and sleepless nights.
